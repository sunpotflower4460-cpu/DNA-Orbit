## Rules

- Every entry needs an owner and objective exit condition.
- Blocker debt cannot be accepted.
- High debt blocks release and must be isolated from shipping scope.
- Repeated debt in one domain escalates the next related change’s risk tier.
- Debt is not removed when inconvenient; move it to Resolved with evidence.
